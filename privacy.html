Privacy Policy

This application is a personal integration used to access and manage Google Drive files through rclone.

Google User Data

Google Drive data accessed through this application is used solely to allow authorized users to manage their own files through rclone.

This application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and access tokens are handled by rclone on the user's own device. This website does not collect or store Google Drive files, authentication credentials, or access tokens.

Use of Data

Google user data is accessed only as necessary to perform file operations requested by the authorized user, such as listing, uploading, downloading, moving, or deleting files.

Contact

This application is maintained for personal use. Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the application's contact email listed in the Google OAuth configuration.
